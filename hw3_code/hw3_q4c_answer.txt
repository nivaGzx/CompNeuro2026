   - Origin of Minor Systematic Difference (~0.96x):

         Delta_L = 2*arctan(200) = 3.13159 < pi.
         Truncating the voltage at +/-200 clips the extreme apex of the spike

     (ii) Higher-Order Corrections: Phase averaging neglects O(eps^2) terms

3. Why a Symmetric Cutoff (+/- L) is Important:
   (a) Zero Net DC Current Bias (Principal Value Cancellation):
       The uncoupled QIF orbit v(t) = -cot(t) has logarithmic singularities
       at threshold (+inf) and reset (-inf). Integrating the gap junction current
       eps*(v_j - v_i) over one cycle yields logarithmic contributions:
           int v_i dt ~ ln(L_th) - ln(|L_r|) = ln(L_th / |L_r|).
       With a symmetric cutoff (L_th = |L_r| = L), ln(L / L) = 0, so the spike upstroke
       and reset downstroke cancel identically, preserving zero net DC bias.
       An asymmetric cutoff (L_th != |L_r|) would inject a spurious net DC offset,
       shifting the intrinsic firing frequency and disrupting phase balance.

   (b) Preservation of Odd Symmetry of the Interaction Function:
       Symmetric cutoff preserves the waveform anti-symmetry v(t - Delta/2) = -v(-t),
       which guarantees that the phase interaction function is strictly odd:
           H(-chi) = -H(chi),   with H(0) = 0.
       This odd symmetry ensures that the in-phase synchronous state (phi = 0)
       remains an exact, unshifted stable fixed point of the coupled dynamics.
